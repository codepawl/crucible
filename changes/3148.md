- raster: rough glass (KHR_materials_transmission with roughness) blurs the scene it refracts; glass bends the ray at two interfaces and marches the bent ray through the opaque scene's depth, so a thick sphere magnifies and flips what lies behind it and an iris behind a cornea shifts and magnifies as in the path tracer; a glTF mesh with transmission and no KHR_materials_volume gets a derived thickness (closed shells, bulging caps; an explicit thickness always wins); the alpha of blended glass no longer leaves an unrefracted ghost under a fully transmissive surface (#3148) A march that misses (the ray ends in the sky or leaves the screen) reads the picture where the ray last was on the screen, and the blur follows the microfacet width (roughness squared), so a glTF cornea of roughness 0.08 stays sharp.
