- Oak, ash, aspen and birch trees grow denser crowns: leaf cards (crossed pairs of about 0.6 m) on the twig generations, turned along the twig and a little up and outward (down for birch), with 256 px twig cards of real-sized leaves; `tree.leafCount` scales the twigs per parent and `tree.leafStart` sets where they begin (#3041, #3057)
- Oak, ash, aspen and birch each grow a habit: a pruning envelope that cuts limbs and twigs where they leave the crown outline, an oak on a half-height trunk with level lower limbs and steep upper ones, a dome for ash and narrow crowns for aspen and birch (#3041, #3057)
