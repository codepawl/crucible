- Material presets for blocky scenes: `material.preset` takes `smoothPlastic`, `wood`, `grass`, `neon`, `glass` or `brick`, fills the fields the material leaves out, tints patterns with the part colour and keeps the grain size in metres; listed in `scene_capabilities` topic `materialPresets`.
- Material presets, round 4: `grass` is sparse upright blades that read as turf on any part colour, `neon` is a glowing panel with a bright tube frame that drives bloom (pair with `effects.bloom` strength 8, threshold 10, radius 16 and a lower exposure over a bright wall), and `glass` refracts what is behind it with a Fresnel edge light (#3234).
- Material presets, round 5: grass blades fade to nothing at the Voronoi cell edge (no hairline cracks), the red tint no longer reads darker than the others, and glass bends the backdrop gently (ior 1.12) with a stronger Fresnel edge and a faint cyan tint so clear glass reads (#3234).
- Material presets, round 6: `glass` is more transparent (transmission 0.7, opacity 0.4) with a gentle bend (ior 1.15, thickness 0.06) so the bevel shows no banded steps, and the docs name `environment.sunAngularRadius` for a soft tinted shadow (#3234).
- Material presets, round 7: `glass` has a stronger Fresnel edge and tint so clear glass reads over light backdrops, a doc note that a round bevel (`bevel` 0.2) shows the bend and avoids the corner marks of the rasterizer's refraction march (#3256), and that the soft sun drops the tinted shadow (#3259); `grass` defaults to a deeper green (#3234).
- Material presets, final: `glass` has no transmission (Glaze drops emission with it, #3279) and draws a thin bright Fresnel rim by `emission` 30, `grass` repeats every 1.25 m across so a 1.2 m cube face shows none, and a path-traced reference sits beside each preset still (#3234).
- Material presets, round 9: `neon` is black metal with emission 80 on a bright frame (face 9.6) so bloom strength 8, threshold 10, radius 16 lifts the ring 8 px outside by 30 levels over a mid-grey wall at exposure 0.38 while the face keeps its hue, and `glass` rim emission is 7 so no channel clips at 255 (#3234).
