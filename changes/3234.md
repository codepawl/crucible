- Material presets for blocky scenes: `material.preset` takes `smoothPlastic`, `wood`, `grass`, `neon`, `glass` or `brick`, fills the fields the material leaves out, tints patterns with the part colour and keeps the grain size in metres; listed in `scene_capabilities` topic `materialPresets`.
- Material presets, round 4: `grass` is sparse upright blades that read as turf on any part colour, `neon` is a glowing panel with a bright tube frame that drives bloom (pair with `effects.bloom` strength 1, threshold 0.8, radius 3 and a point light for spill), and `glass` refracts what is behind it with a Fresnel edge light (#3234).
- Material presets, round 5: grass blades fade to nothing at the Voronoi cell edge (no hairline cracks), the red tint no longer reads darker than the others, and glass bends the backdrop gently (ior 1.12) with a stronger Fresnel edge and a faint cyan tint so clear glass reads (#3234).
