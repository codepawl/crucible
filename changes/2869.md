- A recipe material takes scanned PBR maps: `scene_asset_catalog` lists a folder of standard map names as one `textureSet`, `textureSet: "<name>"` or `colorMap`, `normalMap`, `roughnessMap`, `metallicMap`, `occlusionMap` and `heightMap` (catalog image IDs) fill the material, `normalConvention: "directx"` flips the green channel, and roughness and metallic are repacked into the glTF layout (#2869)
